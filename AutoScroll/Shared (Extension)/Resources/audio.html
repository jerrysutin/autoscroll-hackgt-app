Test your microphone before connecting it to scrolling.

neutral

Microphone is off.

Laughter and chuckling → positive. Crying and groaning → negative. Uncertain sounds → neutral.

Use headphones so the video’s audio is less likely to trigger a reaction. This test recognizes sounds, not the meaning of spoken words.

Audio is processed on your device and is never recorded or uploaded. Keep this tab open while listening; closing it stops the microphone.
